Room studio — every line is a node

A room is just a closed cross-section dragged the length of a corridor. Edit the silhouette on the left — drag the nodes, add new ones on the edges, double-tap to remove — and the camera sees the room rebuild itself in real time. The presets sweep every node from where it is to where the next shape wants it.
